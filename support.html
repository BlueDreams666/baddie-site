Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your devices, and in your own iCloud if you turn on automatic backups or Baddie Pro's iCloud sync. There's no account, so we can't restore it for you: use Settings › Data & Backups to make a backup or export CSV.

Moving to a new iPhone? Restore from your iCloud or computer backup, or export your history as CSV in Settings › Data & Backups and import it on the new phone.

How do I delete everything? Settings › Data & Backups › Erase everything. You get a few seconds to undo it.

Widgets show old numbers. Open Baddie once; widgets refresh from the app. If they still don't, remove and add the widget again.

I bought Baddie Pro but it's not showing. Settings › Get Baddie Pro › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I have a code for Baddie Pro. Settings › Get Baddie Pro › Redeem a code, or open the link that came with the code.

How do I cancel a subscription? In the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Baddie. A lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.

Can I lock the app? Yes: Settings › Face ID & Passcode. It also hides widgets and the Watch while locked.
